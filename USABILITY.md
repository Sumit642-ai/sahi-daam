# Seller usability test — Sahi Daam MVP

Template for the usability test in spec v2, Section 16. **Every answer below is blank on purpose.** Fill it in only from real sessions with real sellers. Never invent or estimate a result.

## 1. Rules

- **Sellers:** minimum 3, target 5.
- **Setting:** on the seller's own phone, in their own language, about 15 minutes each.
- **Roles:** one person runs the test and does not help, hint or explain. One person takes notes.
- **Words:** write the seller's exact words, in quotes.
- **Quoting:** ask permission before quoting anyone. Use first name and city only.

## 2. Tasks and questions

Read each task out exactly as written, in the seller's language. Start the timer when you finish reading.

| Task | English | Hindi |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Check one of your products and tell me how much you actually keep per sale." | "अपना कोई एक product डालिए और बताइए कि हर sale पर आपको असल में कितना बचता है।" |
| T2 | "Meesho asks you to join a sale at 20% off. Should you join?" | "Meesho आपको 20% छूट वाली sale में बुलाता है। आपको join करना चाहिए या नहीं?" |
| T3 | "Upload your Meesho payment report." *(only if they have one)* | "अपनी Meesho payment report upload कीजिए।" |
| T4 | "Which of your products is losing you the most money?" | "आपका कौन सा product आपको सबसे ज़्यादा नुकसान दे रहा है?" |

**Follow-up questions** (ask after the tasks):

| Question | Answer options |
|---|---|
| Q1 "Would you use this every week?" | Yes / Maybe / No |
| Q2 "Would you pay ₹99 a month for a weekly check of all your products?" | Yes / Maybe / No |
| Q3 "Who else should use this?" | Open answer |
